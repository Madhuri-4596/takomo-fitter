            Step 3 / Swing check

            Let's see your rhythm.

            Record three normal swings to help us compare shaft options.

            • Phone face-on, at chest height, about 3 m away.
            • Your whole body and the club in view, top to finish.
            • Plenty of room to swing safely; nobody nearby.

            Separate from tempo

            7-iron clubhead speed

            Tempo is how long your swing takes; it doesn't tell us how fast the club moves. Speed decides the shaft flex, so it comes from a simulator or launch monitor, or from your carry as the kit's fallback.
